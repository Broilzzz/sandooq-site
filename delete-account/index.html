Delete your Sandooq account

Sandooq, by Ahil Ahmed · Last updated 16 November 2026

You can delete your Sandooq account and the data that goes with it at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Sandooq.Sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.Sandooq shows, vault by vault, what will happen.
  3. Hand over shared vaults.If you're the only owner of a vault others are in, pick who takes over.
  4. Confirm twice, then unlock with your phone's lock.Your account is deleted straight away and the app's data on this phone is wiped.

By email, if you can't use the app

Send an email from the Google address you sign in to Sandooq with. Requests from any other address are turned down, so nobody can delete your account for you.

To
ahilahmedhussain15@gmail.com
Subject
Delete my Sandooq account

We'll confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you own a vault other people are in, tell us who should take it over, or we'll ask you.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, Google name and ID, display name and sign-in records.
  • Your public keys and the encrypted backups of your private key.
  • Your vault memberships, your unused invites and your join requests.
  • Vaults only you are in, with all their documents, records and files.
  • On your phone (when you delete in the app): Sandooq's database, saved and original files, your keys, and the copy of your key in your phone's backup.

Vaults you share with others

  • Documents and files you added stay in the vault for the other members. They'll show as added by "a deleted account".
  • You're removed from the vault, and the vault gets a new key, so nothing added later can be opened with your old keys.
  • If you're the only owner, the member you choose becomes the owner.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted database backups made before your deletion still hold your account until they expire, at most 32 days later. They're locked and can't be edited, so we can't remove you from them sooner, but they're never used except to recover from a server failure, and we repeat your deletion if one is ever restored.
  • A note that your account was deleted, with its random ID and the time but no email or name, kept only as long as those backups, for that purpose.
  • Nothing else. Vault content in backups is end-to-end encrypted, and we never hold the keys to it.

Only want to remove some documents? You don't need to delete your account. Owners and admins can delete documents, files or whole vaults in the app. Deleted items stay in the vault's Trash for 30 days, then are removed for good (or straight away if you empty the Trash).

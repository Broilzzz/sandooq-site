Your family's documents. Locked and close at hand.

Passports, Aadhaar, Emirates ID, Iqama, certificates. Keep them in one vault, share it with the people you trust, and find any of them by saying its name. Everything is encrypted on your phone, so not even we can open it.

Say it, find it, send it.

Made for families who keep papers for two countries and three generations.

Find by voice

Say "Amma's passport" in Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, Kannada, Arabic or English, and it opens.

Reads them for you

Scan a card and Sandooq fills in the type, name, number and expiry. The reading happens on your phone, never in the cloud.

Shared with family

Invite people by QR code or WhatsApp. Owners, admins, editors and viewers each get the right amount of access.

Send on WhatsApp

The visa office wants a copy? Two taps and it's on its way, from the document itself.

Works offline

Documents you've opened stay on the phone, encrypted. Changes made offline wait safely and go out when you're back.

Nothing lost by accident

Deleted documents wait in the Trash for 30 days. Exact duplicates are caught before they're saved.

We can't read your documents.

Every vault has its own key, made on the phone that created it. Documents are encrypted before they leave your phone, and the key is shared only with the members of that vault. Our servers store locked boxes they have no key for.

Our servers can't see

  • Your files and scans
  • Document names, types and numbers
  • Whose document it is, expiry dates, the text read from it
  • Vault names
  • Any key that could open them

Our servers can see

  • Your Google email and name
  • Which vaults you're in, and your role
  • How many items there are, their sizes and when they changed
  • Who added or changed an item (by account, not what changed)

Lose your phone? Your recovery key, your phone's own backup, or another member of the vault can bring your access back. The full details are in the privacy policy.

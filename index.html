How it works

From a photo to a locked vault in under a minute.

Sandooq does the filing for you, and every step that touches what's on your documents happens on your own phone.

01

Scan or import

Use the camera's document scanner, or pick a PDF or photo. Big files are shrunk on the phone; the original stays with you.

02

Read on your phone

It recognises passports, Aadhaar, PAN, Emirates ID and Iqama, and fills in whose it is, the number and the expiry. No cloud AI ever sees it.

03

Encrypted with the vault key

The file, its title, the number and the text read from it are encrypted before upload. Every file even gets a key of its own.

04

There for the whole family

It syncs to every member's phone, opens offline once viewed, and goes to WhatsApp in two taps when an office asks for a copy.

End-to-end encrypted

We can't read your documents. Here's the proof.

Flip between what your family sees and what actually reaches our server. Only a few details are left readable, because the app can't work without them, and we tell you which.

  • The vault key is made on the phone that creates the vault, and is only ever shared sealed to each member's own key.
  • A server break-in, a leaked backup or a curious engineer would find only locked boxes.
  • Readable to us: your Google email and name, who's in which vault, and counts, sizes and times. The full list.

If you lose your phone

Three ways back in. None of them through us.

Because we never hold your keys, getting back in on a new phone works through things only you and your family have.

Your recovery key

Given once when you set up. Write it down; we never see it.

Your phone's own backup

Android keeps a copy of your key that only your phone's screen lock can open, and brings it back on a new phone.

Someone in your vault

Any member can give you access again, after a clear "security code changed" warning, so a stolen Google account alone isn't enough.

With none of the three, a vault only you were in can't be opened by anyone, including us. That's what end-to-end encryption means, and the app says so plainly.

Questions

The honest answers.

Can Sandooq read my documents?

No. Files, titles, numbers, the text read from them and vault names are encrypted on your phone with keys our servers never hold. We can see your Google email and name, which vaults you're in, and counts, sizes and times. The privacy policy lists everything.

Is my document text sent to an AI?

No. Text recognition runs on your phone. The optional on-device model is downloaded once over Wi-Fi and runs on the phone too; nothing you scan is sent to a cloud reading or AI service.

Who can see what I add?

Only the members of the vault you add it to, because only their phones hold that vault's key. Each member's role decides whether they can add, edit or delete.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Sign in on the new phone and get your keys back with your recovery key, your phone's backup, or a family member in the vault giving you access again.

Where is my data stored?

Our database is in Mumbai, India. Encrypted files and encrypted backups are kept with Cloudflare in the Asia-Pacific region.

Is it free?

Yes, for now. If paid plans come, joining someone else's vault will stay free, and nothing you've stored is ever deleted or locked if you stop paying.

Is there an iPhone app?

Sandooq is coming to Android first. iPhone is planned.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

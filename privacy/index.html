1. Who we are

Sandooq is a family document vault app made by Ahil Ahmed, an individual developer based in Mangalore, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described here is used, and we're the person to contact about it (see Contact).

This policy covers the Sandooq app for Android and the servers it talks to. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics; your browser loads its font from Google Fonts.

2. How the encryption works

When you sign in, your phone makes your own pair of keys. The private one stays in your phone's secure key storage. Each vault gets its own random key, made on the phone that creates the vault, and that key is shared only by locking a copy of it to each member's public key. Every file has its own key too, locked by the vault key.

Your documents, their titles, the person they belong to, their type, numbers, expiry dates, the text read from them, file names and vault names are all encrypted with these keys before they leave your phone. Our servers only ever receive the locked result, and they never hold a key that could open it. The encryption uses the widely reviewed libsodium library.

Signing in with Google only proves who you are. It never creates, holds or unlocks a key.

4. What stays on your phone

Some things never leave your phone at all, not even encrypted:

  • Pictures used as previews of your documents.
  • The original, uncompressed file when Sandooq stores a smaller copy.
  • What you type or say into search.
  • Your settings: language, theme and app lock.

Everything Sandooq keeps on your phone is in an encrypted database or encrypted files. Decrypted documents are only held in memory, or in short-lived files that are wiped after use.

5. What other members of your vaults see

The people in a vault with you can see your display name, Google name, email address, role and security code, and which items you added, changed or deleted. Because they hold the vault's key, they can open its documents, including the ones you added, and they can save copies or send them out of the app. Only invite people you trust.

An invite link or QR code only creates a request to join. Nobody gets in until an owner or admin lets them in from a phone that holds the vault's key.

7. Voice search

Voice search uses your phone's own speech recognition service (on most Android phones, Google's). What you say is turned into text by that service under its own privacy policy, and depending on your phone and language it may be processed on Google's servers. Sandooq only receives the text, matches it against your documents on the phone, and doesn't save it or send it to us. The microphone is only used while voice search is listening.

8. Files you send out of the app

When you choose to send or export a document (for example to WhatsApp, or to a website's upload button), that copy leaves Sandooq unencrypted, and what happens to it is covered by the receiving app's or website's policy. Sandooq never does this by itself; it always takes your tap and confirmation.

9. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and invite QR codes.
  • Microphone: only while voice search is listening.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: for the optional app lock and to confirm sensitive steps. This is checked by your phone; nothing about it reaches us.
  • Internet and network state: to sync, and to download the reading model only on Wi-Fi.

10. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for their own use.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't profile you.
  • We don't send your documents or their text to any cloud reading or AI service.
  • We don't merge accounts automatically based on email address.

We will only disclose data if the law requires it. Even then we can only hand over what's readable in section 3; we can't decrypt vault content.

11. How long we keep data

  • Your account and vaults: until you delete them.
  • Deleted documents and files: kept in the vault's Trash for 30 days so they can be restored, then removed for good. Emptying the Trash removes them sooner.
  • Encrypted database backups: made twice a month and kept for about 30 days (at most 32), then deleted automatically. Deleted data and deleted accounts can stay in a backup until it expires.
  • Service health totals: 120 days.
  • Records of deleted accounts: after an account is deleted we keep a note of its random account ID and the deletion time (no email or name) for as long as our backups last, so the deletion can be repeated if we ever have to restore from a backup.
  • Technical server logs kept by our providers: a few days.

12. Deleting your data

You can delete single documents, files and vaults in the app. To delete your whole account, go to Profile → Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page if you can't use the app. Vaults only you are in are deleted with everything in them. In vaults shared with others, what you added stays for the other members, you're removed, and the vault gets a new key.

13. Security and your recovery key

Besides end-to-end encryption, every request to our servers checks your session and your role in the vault, file links expire within minutes, and backups are encrypted before they're stored. The data on your phone is in an encrypted database.

When you set up Sandooq you get a recovery key. We never see it. Keep it somewhere safe: anyone who has it and your Google account could restore your keys, and if you lose your phone without the recovery key, your phone's backup or another vault member, a vault only you were in can't be opened again, by anyone, including us.

If we learn of a breach affecting your personal data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

14. Payments

Sandooq is free for now. If we offer paid plans, payment will go through Google Play. We would receive your plan and its status, never your card or bank details.

15. Children

Sandooq accounts are for adults (18 or older). Parents and guardians may keep their children's documents in their own vaults. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the UAE's and Saudi Arabia's personal data protection laws, and the GDPR, you can:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it (you can change your display name in the app at any time);
  • delete it, or withdraw your consent, by deleting your account;
  • nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself;
  • complain to us, and then to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

Email us from the address you sign in with and we'll reply within 30 days. We can't export or read your encrypted vault content for you; use the app's export or send options to get copies of your documents.

17. Where data is stored

Our database is in Mumbai, India, and encrypted files and backups are in Cloudflare's Asia-Pacific region. The backup job runs on GitHub's servers, which may be outside India (usually in the USA), and Google's services may process data in other countries. By using Sandooq you agree to these transfers, which are protected by the measures described in this policy.
